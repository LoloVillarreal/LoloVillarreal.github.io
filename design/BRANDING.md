## Koala L&A

Generado con la herramienta integrada ImageGen; original en `design/koala-la-source.png`, recurso preparado en `public/plan-la-koala.png`, e iconos derivados de 180, 192 y 512 px, con versión maskable. Se recortaron los márgenes transparentes para mejorar la legibilidad, se renovaron las rutas de iconos y se actualizaron también favicon e icono SVG. Las rutas anteriores se conservan como alias compatibles. Animación suave mediante CSS; reduce movimiento la desactiva.

Prompt final:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a single polished cute illustrated koala mascot logo for a personal fitness and everyday wellness PWA named PLAN Lolo & alexia. Very tender smiling baby koala, round soft grey ears, tiny black eyes, rosy cheeks, warm friendly expression, seated and facing forward holding a small muted sage-green heart-shaped cushion with the exact clearly legible lettering 'L&A' in cream. Minimal cozy storybook illustration with clean rounded edges, soft gentle shading, sage green and warm grey palette; adult-friendly, refined and sweet. Subject centered with generous transparent margins, compact square composition that remains readable as an app icon. Entire koala including ears and feet visible. True transparent background, no backdrop, no surrounding text, no watermark. This image will be gently animated in CSS in the application; provide a single static neutral mascot pose.

## Comidas

Generadas con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. Original: `design/food-ideas-source.png`; ocho celdas preparadas en `public/food/0.jpg` a `7.jpg`. Fotografías ilustrativas, independientes del peso de ingredientes del menú.

Prompt final:

> Create a polished editorial food photography contact sheet for a Spanish wellness meal inspiration guide. Exact layout: 4 equal columns by 2 equal rows, eight photographs in a seamless regular grid with absolutely no gutters or margins, each image contained entirely in its own equal cell, no text, no letters, no labels. Landscape wide canvas. Same soft natural daylight, warm cream tabletop and muted sage ceramics across all eight cells. Each dish seen from a high three-quarter angle, complete dish centered and clearly separated from adjacent tiles. Top row left to right: 1 natural yogurt bowl with berries and walnuts; 2 wholegrain toast with crushed tomato and fresh cheese; 3 warm oatmeal porridge with banana slices and peanut butter; 4 scrambled eggs on wholegrain toast with orange wedges. Bottom row left to right: 5 rice bowl with grilled chicken and varied vegetables; 6 lentil salad with tomato carrot and greens; 7 baked salmon with potato and green vegetables; 8 fresh apple wedges, a small plain yogurt and a handful of almonds as a snack. Realistic appetizing food, ordinary home cooking, no calorie numbers, no exaggerated diet imagery, no people, no watermarks.
